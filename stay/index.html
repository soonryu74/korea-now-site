Where to stay in Seoul

Myeong-dong 명동

Best for: A first visit — shopping, street food, and everything else a short ride away. The most central place to sleep. Palaces, Namsan Tower, Dongdaemun and the markets are all within 15 minutes.

Getting around
Line 4 Myeong-dong · Line 2 Euljiro 1-ga · 10 min walk to City Hall
From the airport
Airport limousine bus stops here; or AREX to Seoul Station, then one stop on Line 4
At night
Lively at night
Price
₩₩ Mid-range
Worth knowing
Very touristy — many repeat visitors skip it for sightseeing, since the shops are found elsewhere too. As a base it is still hard to beat. The street-food lanes are packed until late; rooms in older hotels are small.

Jongno & Insadong 종로·인사동

Best for: Palaces and old Seoul on foot. Gyeongbokgung, Changdeokgung, Bukchon and Ikseon-dong are walking distance. Hanok guesthouses in the alleys.

Getting around
Lines 1, 3, 5 Jongno 3-ga · Line 3 Anguk
From the airport
Airport limousine bus; or AREX to Seoul Station and Line 1
At night
Quiet at night
Price
₩₩ Mid-range
Worth knowing
Quieter after 22:00 — fewer late-night food options than Myeong-dong or Hongdae.

Hongdae 홍대

Best for: Nightlife, live music, and the cheapest beds. Young, busy, lots of hostels. The one neighbourhood with a direct train to Incheon Airport and still something to do at 2 am.

Getting around
Line 2, Gyeongui-Jungang and AREX — all at Hongik Univ. station
From the airport
AREX all-stop train runs straight to Hongik Univ. — no transfer
At night
Loud at night
Price
₩ Budget
Worth knowing
Friday and Saturday nights are loud until morning. Ask for a room away from the main streets.

Seoul Station 서울역

Best for: Early flights, late arrivals, or taking the KTX to Busan. Pure convenience. The airport express leaves from the station, and so do the fast trains to Busan and Gyeongju.

Getting around
Lines 1 & 4, AREX, and the KTX to Busan and Gyeongju
From the airport
AREX express, non-stop to Incheon Airport, from here
At night
Quiet at night
Price
₩₩ Mid-range
Worth knowing
Not much to do in the evening — you will ride into Myeong-dong (one stop) to eat.

Seochon 서촌

Best for: A quiet, local-feeling stay next to the main palace. Hanok alleys on the west side of Gyeongbokgung, with small cafés and Tongin Market. Far calmer than Bukchon.

Getting around
Line 3 Gyeongbokgung
From the airport
Airport limousine bus to Gwanghwamun; or AREX to Seoul Station and a short taxi
At night
Quiet at night
Price
₩₩ Mid-range
Worth knowing
Mostly guesthouses and hanok stays; few big hotels.

Gangnam 강남

Best for: Business trips, modern hotels, COEX and Lotte World. Newer, bigger hotels, department stores and the Starfield Library. Close to Lotte World Tower.

Getting around
Line 2 Gangnam · Sinbundang line
From the airport
Airport limousine bus to the major hotels
At night
Lively at night
Price
₩₩₩ Higher-end
Worth knowing
About 30–40 minutes by subway from the palaces and old Seoul.

Itaewon 이태원

Best for: Food from everywhere and an easy place to be foreign. The most international neighbourhood in Seoul, with halal food, English everywhere and Leeum museum nearby.

Getting around
Line 6 Itaewon
From the airport
Airport limousine bus; or AREX to Seoul Station and a taxi
At night
Lively at night
Price
₩₩ Mid-range
Worth knowing
Hilly streets — heavy luggage is hard work.

Where to stay in Busan

Haeundae 해운대

Best for: A beach holiday with big hotels. The famous beach, BUSAN X the SKY, and Blueline Park. Resort hotels along the sand.

Getting around
Line 2 Haeundae · Donghae line
From the airport
About an hour from Gimhae Airport by limousine bus, or light rail + Line 2
At night
Lively at night
Price
₩₩₩ Higher-end
Worth knowing
Summer weekends are the busiest place in the country.

Gwangalli 광안리

Best for: The bridge view at night, calmer than Haeundae. The lit-up Gwangan Bridge from your window, cafés along the beach, and Saturday drone shows in season.

Getting around
Line 2 Gwangan or Geumnyeonsan
From the airport
Light rail + Line 2, about 50 minutes
At night
Lively at night
Price
₩₩ Mid-range

Seomyeon 서면

Best for: Getting everywhere in Busan cheaply. Busan’s downtown. Two subway lines, the cheapest rooms, and food until late.

Getting around
Lines 1 & 2 — the main transfer station
From the airport
Light rail to Sasang, then Line 2 — about 40 minutes
At night
Loud at night
Price
₩ Budget
Worth knowing
No sea view, and the bar streets are noisy on weekends.

Nampo & Jagalchi 남포

Best for: Markets, old Busan, and the KTX. Jagalchi fish market, BIFF Square and Gukje Market on your doorstep; Gamcheon Culture Village is a short bus ride.

Getting around
Line 1 Nampo · Busan Station (KTX) is 3 stops
From the airport
Airport limousine bus to Nampo; or light rail + Line 2 + Line 1
At night
Lively at night
Price
₩ Budget

Where to stay in Jeju

Jeju City (near the airport) 제주시 (연동·노형)

Best for: Short stays, no car, and late or early flights. Restaurants, Dongmun Market and the bus terminal. The easiest base if you are not renting a car.

Getting around
Most island bus routes start here
From the airport
10–15 minutes by taxi or bus
At night
Lively at night
Price
₩₩ Mid-range
Worth knowing
Everything scenic is 1 hour or more away by bus.

Seogwipo 서귀포

Best for: Waterfalls, the south coast and Olle walks. Cheonjiyeon and Jeongbang falls, Olle Route 7 and Jungmun resort beaches are all close.

Getting around
Airport limousine buses run to the main hotels
From the airport
About 1 hour by limousine bus or car
At night
Quiet at night
Price
₩₩ Mid-range

Seongsan (east coast) 성산

Best for: Sunrise at Seongsan Ilchulbong and the ferry to Udo. Stay the night and climb the peak for sunrise before the tour buses arrive; the Udo ferry is five minutes away.

Getting around
Bus 201 along the coast; a car is much easier
From the airport
About 1 hour 10 minutes by car
At night
Quiet at night
Price
₩₩ Mid-range
Worth knowing
Very few restaurants open after 20:00.

Where to stay in Gyeongju

Hwangnidan-gil 황리단길

Best for: Walking to the royal tombs and Donggung Palace at night. Hanok cafés and guesthouses next to Daereungwon; Donggung and Wolji, lit up after dark, is a 15-minute walk.

Getting around
Bus from Singyeongju KTX station, about 30 minutes
From the airport
Nearest is Gimhae (Busan) or Pohang — most people come by KTX
At night
Quiet at night
Price
₩₩ Mid-range
Worth knowing
Bulguksa and Seokguram are 30–40 minutes away by bus.

Where to stay in Jeonju

Jeonju Hanok Village 전주한옥마을

Best for: A night in a traditional hanok. Sleeping in a hanok is the point: floor mattresses, a courtyard, and the village empty and quiet after the day visitors leave.

Getting around
Bus or taxi from Jeonju KTX station, about 20 minutes
From the airport
No airport nearby — KTX from Seoul (Yongsan) is about 1 h 40 min
At night
Quiet at night
Price
₩₩ Mid-range
Worth knowing
Thin walls and floor bedding — not for everyone.
