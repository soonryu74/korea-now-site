When to go

There is no weekly closing day here — you can come any day of the week.

Counting foreign visitors to Mokpo-si, Jeonnam over twelve weeks, Monday is the quietest day you can actually go and Wednesday the busiest — a difference of about 50%. These are district-wide figures rather than a headcount at the gate, so treat them as a tendency.

Opening hours are 09:00 to 20:00. [Summer]Monday-Thursday Friday-Sunday & public holidays 09:00-21:00[Winter]Monday-Thursday 09:00-19:00Friday-…. It stays open into the evening, which is usually the calmest and coolest time to come. Tour groups tend to arrive mid-morning and again after lunch; the first hour after opening is normally the quietest stretch of any day.

What it costs, and what to bring

Adult admission is ₩24,000 — Standard cabin, return. Crystal-floor cabin costs more. By the standards of most countries this is very cheap, and it is charged at the gate rather than online, so there is no need to book ahead.

Cards are accepted. Foreign Visa and Mastercard work almost everywhere in Korea, and contactless is normal.

English signage is partial — enough to find your way, not enough to explain what you are looking at. The Korean name above is worth showing to a taxi driver or a passer-by; it works better than pronouncing it.

Getting there

There is no subway station close by. A taxi or an intercity bus is usually the way in — show the Korean name above to the driver.
